AI-UX Cost Optimization

Make intelligent experiences cost a fraction to run.

Most AI products are built to impress in a demo and quietly bankrupt in production — bloated prompts, redundant model calls, and design patterns that burn tokens for no user value. I re-architect the experience so it stays magical while the bill collapses.

§ 04

The system underneath

// a closed loop — observe, infer, decide, explain, learn

Every surface you just used is one output of the same loop. Design the loop well and personalization stops being a pile of features and becomes a single, legible system.

01 · SENSE
Behavioral signals

Dwell, open, save, dismiss, buy — weighted by intent. Implicit and explicit signal captured with consent.

→
02 · INFER
Profile model

An 8-dimension affinity vector updated by decayed, confidence-weighted learning. This is the user profile.

→
03 · DECIDE
Recommendation

Relevance × novelty × diversity, tuned by the explore/exploit dial. Ranks the surface each tick.

→
04 · EXPLAIN
Reasoning layer

Every decision decomposes into contributions, plain language, and a counterfactual. Trust before delight.

→
05 · REMEMBER
Memory + control

Confident facts persist; the user can edit, pause, or forget. The loop closes back to SENSE.

§ 05

The behavioral science

// personalization is applied psychology — designed on purpose
Autonomy & control

The IKEA effect, for your own profile

People trust — and value — systems they help build. The preference studio and the "forget me" control convert a passive recipient into a co-author. Perceived control raises tolerance for the occasional wrong guess.

Transparency

The explanation is the product

Unexplained accuracy reads as surveillance; explained accuracy reads as service. The "why", the contribution bars, and the counterfactual exist because legibility is what turns a creepy guess into a helpful one.

Cold start & habituation

Earn the right to personalize

The surface opens on popularity, not presumption, then shifts as evidence accrues — and the explore dial fights filter-bubble fatigue. Adaptation is paced to how trust is actually built: gradually, then visibly.

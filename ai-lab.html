Galaxy 02// The efficiency thesis

Everyone is chasing capability. I argue the real advantage is efficiency — People, Planet, Profit. The teams that win won’t have the biggest model; they’ll have the one that’s cheapest to run at quality. Triage, route, and measure — every additional token has a cost.

Galaxy 03// The trust stand

Trust — clever is easy, trusted is the work

AI’s biggest enterprise failure isn’t intelligence — it’s trust. As interfaces get more persuasive, the line between an ethical nudge and a dark pattern gets thinner. I design for trust before delight, and I build the tools that make manipulation — and honest uncertainty — visible.

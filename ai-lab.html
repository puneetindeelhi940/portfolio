X.01 · LIVE FEATURED PROJECT · VIBE-CODED 2025

Design Intelligence Portal

A Design AI Intelligence Platform that helps teams learn prompt engineering, optimize AI usage, benchmark performance, and measure organizational AI maturity — turning a fragmented individual skill into a scaled organizational capability.

01
Prompt as Org Capability, Not Individual Skill
Centralised learning + execution: discover, reuse, and improve prompts collectively. Reduces duplication. Standardises best practices. Accelerates designer onboarding.
02
AI Usage Visibility — The Leadership Dashboard
Prompt usage trends, team adoption rates, model efficiency scores, and high-performing patterns — all surfaced for leaders. Shifts the portal from “tool” to management intelligence system. Answers the question every exec asks: “Are we investing in AI effectively?”
03
Gamified Adoption via Leaderboards & Performance Signals
Best prompt creators, AI efficiency champions, top contributors — surfaced through recognition and healthy competition. Turns AI learning into culture, not compliance. Enterprise change management gold.
04
Prompt Coaching — From Average to Expert Prompting
Benchmarks prompt effectiveness, suggests better structures, compares outputs across models. Less trial-and-error → faster outputs → measurable quality gain. No expensive training required.
05
Enterprise AI ROI & Governance for Design Teams
Which models deliver value? Where are inefficiencies? Which teams are getting ROI? Brings visibility, accountability, and measurable governance to enterprise AI adoption — not anecdotes.
// STRATEGIC LENS · WHY THIS EXISTS
This portal is the executable version of what I led inside Dell’s Experience Design Group as the AI-Design & Innovation CoE. The insight: most enterprise AI adoption fails not from lack of tools, but from lack of shared standards, visibility, and behavioural incentives. Stop scattered prompting — build an AI-native design culture.

§ 02 VIGYAN SETU — UX CASE // IndiaAI Impact Summit 2026 · read as a design problem
FEB 19–20 · BHARAT MANDAPAM

India is hosting IndiaAI Impact Summit 2026 — a flagship global convening on responsible AI for the public interest. The organising idea, Vigyan Setu (“bridge of science”), frames the event around three sutras — People, Planet, Progress — and seven chakras of action.

I’ve been reading this as a UX case in itself: the design problem of making a seven-track, eleven-ministry, citizen-facing convening legible to a non-specialist — without simplifying the policy underneath. What follows is my read of the design problem, the frame, and what a thoughtful UX response would look like.

01
Human Capital
AI talent, skilling, livelihoods at the scale of a billion.
02
Inclusion
Multilingual, accessible, equitable AI for every Indian.
03
Safe & Trusted AI
Evaluation, guardrails, content authenticity.
04
Science & Research
Frontier work in foundation models, biology, climate, materials.
05
Resilience
Compute, energy, supply, security — the substrate.
06
Democratising Resources
Open compute, datasets, models accessible to startups, academia, government.
07
AI for Growth
Productivity, MSMEs, agriculture, services — economic uplift.
// THE DESIGN PROBLEM

Seven tracks, eleven ministries, hundreds of citizen-facing services, two days at Bharat Mandapam, and a global press audience. The risk is that every track speaks its own language, every demo lives on its own URL, and the citizen leaves with a slogan instead of a map.

The design opportunity is the same as Dell.com’s, at a different scale: multiple buyers, one URL. Researchers, policymakers, founders, and citizens are all in the audience. Each needs a different entry point, but they need to walk away believing in the same story.

// THE UX RESPONSE I’D BUILD
  • An editorial spine — three sutras as the navigational frame, not seven competing tracks.
  • A role-aware entry — researcher, policymaker, founder, citizen. The same content reframed for each.
  • Demos as artefacts, not slides — every chakra surfaced through a working citizen-facing experience.
  • A post-summit afterlife: the demos become a public catalogue, not a one-day spectacle.

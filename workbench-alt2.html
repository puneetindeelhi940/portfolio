AI Efficiency Workbench · A Puneet Arora build

Enterprise AI without audit is a leak.

Most enterprises route every task to the most expensive model — out of caution. The expensive part isn't the bill. It's that nobody can explain, after the fact, why a regulated workload ran on a frontier API, or why a classification job spent five figures last quarter when a model 30× cheaper would have done it better.

AI Efficiency Workbench is the operating layer between your people and your models. The first module — Routing — turns every model decision into something visible, auditable, defensible. Hospital triage for AI workloads. More modules to follow.

Chapter 09 — Anticipated Questions
What the Board Will Ask

AI-predicted challenging questions executives might raise during the review, with suggested data-backed responses.

❓ Why not launch earlier in Q2?
+
Simulation data shows Q2 launch with current scope would yield only 61% delivery confidence — below our 75% threshold. The Q3 timeline accommodates scope reduction and team scaling, improving confidence to 84%. The 2-month delay protects us from a costly post-launch recovery cycle.
❓ Why increase the engineering budget by 60%?
+
The investment produces +22% ROI improvement and +18% velocity gain. At ₹80L, we achieve capital efficiency of ₹3.6L per percentage point of confidence gain — well within industry benchmarks for enterprise SaaS delivery. The alternative (maintaining ₹50L) carries a 40% probability of delivery slip.
❓ What happens if revenue targets are missed?
+
The strategy is resilient to revenue underperformance. Medium scope reduces our exposure: if Q3 revenue comes in 15% below target, the product still delivers positive ROI within 18 months. The modular architecture allows further scope reduction without architectural rework.
❓ Why defer Feature C to Phase 2?
+
Feature C adds 3.2 weeks to the critical path and requires a specialized ML engineer we haven't hired yet. Including it would drop delivery confidence from 84% to approximately 67%. Our pilot customers rank Features A and B 2.4x higher in priority. Feature C is roadmapped for Q4 with dedicated resourcing.
❓ What assumptions have the highest uncertainty?
+
Three assumptions carry elevated uncertainty: (1) New hire ramp-up time — we assume 4 weeks, but industry average is 6-8 weeks; (2) Enterprise pilot conversion rate — our 35% assumption is based on comparable products; (3) API integration timeline — dependent on third-party vendor responsiveness. We've built 2 weeks of buffer for each.

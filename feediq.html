THE PROBLEM

Cargill tests 390,000 samples a year.
The insight dies in a PDF.

Cargill's 2025 Global Mycotoxin Report analyzes nearly 390,000 samples across 41 countries. But feed buyers, livestock producers, and nutritionists have no interactive tool to assess their specific risk or act on it.

71% CONTAMINATION RATE

71% of global feed samples test positive for at least one mycotoxin above detection limits. This isn't an edge case — it's the baseline.

47% MULTI-TOXIN SAMPLES

Nearly half of all samples contain 3 or more mycotoxins simultaneously, creating compounding risks that single-toxin testing misses.

34% ABOVE RISK THRESHOLD

Over a third of samples exceed Cargill's performance-based risk thresholds — levels linked to measurable productivity losses in livestock.

HOW IT WORKS

From question to optimized feed in seconds

FeedIQ combines large language models, constraint optimization, and Cargill's proprietary mycotoxin intelligence to deliver actionable formulations.

01

Describe your needs

Specify species, growth stage, climate, budget, and any constraints in natural language. No spreadsheet expertise needed.

02

AI parses & optimizes

The LLM extracts requirements, maps them to nutritional constraints, and runs cost optimization against local ingredient databases.

03

Risk overlay applied

Each ingredient is scored against real-time mycotoxin data for its sourcing region. High-risk ingredients trigger automatic alternatives.

04

Review & adjust

Explore cost-risk trade-offs with interactive sliders. Export the final formulation as a production-ready spec sheet.

🚀
Why this matters for Cargill: This product sits at the intersection of three Cargill pain points — mycotoxin risk intelligence (389K samples/year with no interactive tool), feed formulation complexity (their Galleon™ and CNS tools address this but lack natural language interfaces), and the post-restructuring knowledge gap (8,000 departing employees taking formulation expertise with them). FeedIQ captures that expertise in an AI system that scales.
DESIGN RATIONALE

UX decisions and why they matter

CONVERSATIONAL OVER FORMS

Feed nutritionists think in constraints, not spreadsheet cells. A natural language interface matches their mental model: "I need X for Y conditions" — not "fill row 47, column C." This reduces onboarding from days to minutes.

RISK-AWARE BY DEFAULT

Every formulation automatically checks mycotoxin risk. This isn't a separate workflow — it's embedded. Designers call this "safety by architecture." Users don't need to remember to check; the system checks for them.

TRADE-OFF TRANSPARENCY

AI systems fail when they're black boxes. FeedIQ shows the why — cost-risk sliders let users explore alternatives, see what changes, and build trust in the recommendation. Inspired by Cargill's own "explain the trade-off" philosophy.

D.003
2023–26
Dell · Experience Design Group
ORG · DESIGN LEADERSHIP
AI-Design & Innovation Centre of Excellence
Conceptualised and led the AI-Design & Innovation CoE for Dell’s Experience Design Group. The brief: turn AI fluency into an org capability, not a workshop deck. Prompt rituals, evaluation rubrics, sandbox tooling, and a quarterly cadence that put working examples in front of designers rather than slide-decks about AI.

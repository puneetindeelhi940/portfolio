Why publish the working, not the result

A finished artefact proves a model can produce output. It says nothing about whether the person holding it can tell good output from plausible output — which, now that generation is free, is the entire remaining skill.

The margin notes are the portfolio. They show where I applied twenty years of pattern-matching to overrule a fluent answer, and they are the one thing on any of my pages that a model could not have produced on my behalf.

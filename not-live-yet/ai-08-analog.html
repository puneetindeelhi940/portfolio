Concept 08 · a broadside

ON THE ONLY THING LEFT THAT IS SCARCE

Judgment
is the
bottleneck

Twenty years of being wrong in expensive places, offered as a qualification.

❦ ❦ ❦

Generation is now free. Anyone can produce a competent interface, a plausible taxonomy, a confident answer, a portfolio site with particles drifting behind the hero. The supply of adequate output has gone vertical, and its price has gone to nothing.

What has not become cheaper is knowing which of those outputs is hollow. That capacity is not generated. It is accumulated — one costly misjudgment at a time, in rooms where being wrong had a price attached.

I have been wrong in a printer, in an aircraft, in a hospital system, and on a storefront.

Each of those taught something a model cannot hand you, because the lesson was not in the artefact — it was in the consequence. Firmware taught that a control you cannot undo is a different species from one you can. Aviation taught that the failure state is the design. Healthcare at national scale taught that the edge case is a person. Commerce taught that a metric which wins the quarter can lose the customer, and that the two are rarely reported by the same team.

I have spent the last five years building generative systems at Dell — a design practice from nothing, a prompt library, intent estimation, layouts that reshape around what a person appears to want. I am not sceptical of any of it. I am precise about it, which is a different posture and a rarer one.

This page has no animation, no gradient, no assistant waiting in the corner to answer questions about me. That is the argument. In a market where every candidate can now generate an impressive surface, the only remaining signal is what a person chooses to leave out.

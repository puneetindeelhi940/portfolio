Why I built this instead of a case study

A case study shows you an outcome that already worked. It cannot show you the moment before — when the mandate was loud, the evidence was thin, and the obvious move was the wrong one. That moment is the whole job, and it is the only thing a hiring conversation is really trying to get at.

So this page hands you the decisions first. You commit, then I answer. Whether we agree matters far less than whether the reasoning holds up once you can see it. So each answer also carries the sharpest objection I could construct against it, and my response — including the one where the objection is simply better than what I did.

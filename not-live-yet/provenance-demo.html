Provenance Kit — what gets added to each signature tool

Understated on the surface, provable underneath. Every marker below is either invisible-inside-visible-content or in a non-indexed channel, so there is zero SEO penalty surface. Nothing here changes how a visitor experiences the page.

01 The visible layer — understated credit

Your pages already carry a small footer credit. It stays understated; I only make it consistent and add a quiet rights line. This is the only thing a normal visitor could notice.

© 2026 Puneet Arora · Original work · All rights reserved.

No banners, no “DO NOT COPY” warnings, no popups — those read as defensive to recruiters. Just a confident one-line credit.

02 Authorship metadata SEO-positive

Machine-readable authorship in the <head>. Search engines read this as legitimate authorship signals — it helps, never hurts.

<!-- in <head> -->
<meta name="author" content="Puneet Arora">
<meta name="copyright" content="© 2026 Puneet Arora. All rights reserved.">
<link rel="author" href="https://puneetindeelhi940.github.io/portfolio/bio.html">

03 Structured data — JSON-LD SEO-positive (validated)

A CreativeWork record stating author, copyright holder, and publish year. This is the piece that puts a dated authorship claim into a format Google actively understands. I validate it before every push so it never throws a Search Console warning.

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "CreativeWork",
  "name": "Decision Replay™ & Leadership Mode™",
  "url": "https://puneetindeelhi940.github.io/portfolio/decision-replay.html",
  "author": { "@type": "Person", "name": "Puneet Arora" },
  "creator": { "@type": "Person", "name": "Puneet Arora" },
  "copyrightHolder": { "@type": "Person", "name": "Puneet Arora" },
  "copyrightYear": 2026,
  "copyrightNotice": "© 2026 Puneet Arora. All rights reserved.",
  "creativeWorkStatus": "Published"
}
</script>

05 What this does and doesn’t protect

Protects: your exact pages, wording, layout, and code — copying becomes traceable and your authorship provable with a dated record.

Does not protect: the idea. Someone can absorb a concept and rebuild it in their own words — provenance can’t stop that. It defends your pages, not your concepts.

Reality check: this is a detection + proof system, not a lock. It catches copiers and wins the “who was first” argument. Acting on a catch still means a takedown or a message from you.

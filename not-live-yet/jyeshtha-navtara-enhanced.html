Mantra / Deity: Lord Ganesha (Sankat Nashan Stotram)

Vipat Tara Remedies 3rd, 12th, 21st

Transits: Purva Ashadha, Bharani, Purva Phalguni

Danam: Guda Danam (Jaggery, unrefined sugar, medicines)

Danam: Lavana / Ghee Danam (Rock salt or clarified butter)

Mantra / Deity: Lord Vishnu / Narasimha (Vishnu Sahasranama)

Protocol: Double-check paperwork, build buffer time into schedules.

Transits: Shatabhisha, Ardra, Swati

Danam: Tila Sahita Suvarna Danam (Sesame seeds with gold / dark items)

Mantra / Deity: Lord Shiva (Rudra Ashtakam) / Devi Kalratri

Protocol: Limit actions strictly to demolition, purging, or ending harmful ties.

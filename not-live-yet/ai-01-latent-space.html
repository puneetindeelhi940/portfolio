Option 01 · concept

i don't have a résumé.
i have a latent space.

A CV is a list sorted by date — the least useful ordering for the question you actually have, which is "can he do the thing I need?" So this page doesn't sort by date. Type the role you're hiring for and twenty years re-ranks itself against your intent, live, in the browser. That re-ranking is the same primitive I shipped at Dell: intent estimation driving adaptive layout.

Ranked by cosine similarity

Why this, and not a timeline

Every hiring manager arrives with a different query vector. A timeline forces all of them through the same linear scan. Re-ranking respects the reader's intent — and quietly proves I understand embeddings well enough to build the interaction, not just name-drop it.

What's actually running

A hand-built term–vector model over 24 career nodes, cosine-scored against your query, driving position, scale and opacity. No API, no key, no network call. It works on a plane. Under a hundred lines of JavaScript — the restraint is the point.

The tell

8 granted patents · 33 designers · 5 time zones · 20 years. Numbers a recruiter can verify, positioned as coordinates rather than bullets.

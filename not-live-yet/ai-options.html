A note on what these deliberately avoid. No gradient hero, no floating neural-network particles, no "Ask my résumé anything" chatbot. That last one is now the default move, which makes it the opposite of a differentiator — and a RAG box over a two-page PDF demonstrates a tutorial, not twenty years. Every concept here instead borrows a format from the AI field itself and earns its credibility structurally.

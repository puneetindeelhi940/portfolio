One deliberate restraint. Motion here is always attached to meaning — the cards clatter because a departure board clatters, the graph moves because a career genuinely does reorganise. None of it is ambient decoration, and every page still holds up with animation switched off. That distinction is the difference between a designer who can use motion and one who reaches for it.

Puneet Arora · round two

Four things that are not
a résumé in AI costume.

Round one varied the costume — the same document, dressed as four AI artefacts. This round varies the form: who holds the agency, what the medium is, and what the page is actually for. One is a game you lose. One is a free tool that never mentions hiring until the footer. One is a transcript. One refuses to be interactive at all.

05 · YOU PLAY

Run My Org

A playable quarter. 33 designers, 4 products, a GenAI mandate, 13 weeks. You allocate; the sim shows what breaks; then it reveals what he actually did and what it cost.

Medium: simulation, not document.
Agency: the visitor's — you are the one being tested.
Proves: leadership judgment, which bullets cannot carry.

06 · YOU GAIN

The AI Feature Pre-Mortem

A genuinely useful free tool. Describe your AI feature; get back the failure modes that will bite you, ranked, with mitigations. No sign-up. His name appears once, at the bottom.

Medium: instrument, not portfolio.
Posture: value first — earns attention instead of asking for it.
Proves: expertise, by being useful before being seen.
Use the tool →
07 · YOU WATCH

The Margin

A working session with a model, published with his margin notes — including the moment he rejects a fluent, confident, completely hollow answer. The annotations are the portfolio.

Medium: annotated transcript.
Shows: taste in directing a model, not using one.
Proves: the one thing a model could not have written for him.

08 · YOU STOP

Judgment Is the Bottleneck

A letterpress broadside. No animation, no gradient, no assistant in the corner. Argues his AI credibility precisely by refusing every AI aesthetic — because when generation is free, restraint is the signal.

Medium: print object, rendered in a browser.
Interaction: none. Deliberately. Zero scripts.
Proves: taste, and the confidence to leave things out.

Round one, for reference. Four AI-format documents — Latent Space, Context Window, Red Team and Model Card. Red Team still earns its place; the other three are one idea in three outfits, which is the critique that produced this round.

What all four still refuse. No gradient hero, no drifting neural-network particles, and no "Ask my résumé anything" chatbot. That last one has become the default move, which is precisely what disqualifies it — a RAG box over a two-page PDF demonstrates a weekend tutorial, not twenty years.

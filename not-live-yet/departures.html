Why a board, and not a timeline

A timeline implies each posting ended and the next began. A board is closer to the truth: every row is still departing somewhere. The reversibility rule I learned writing printer firmware is the same rule I applied to AI agents twenty years later. The failure-state discipline from aviation is why the Dell chatbot had a designed dead end before it had a happy path.

The status column is the honest part. Five rows read on time. One reads delayed, and it is the one I would most want to be asked about.

About NETRADEFEND

concept prototype · simulated data

A cinematic cyber-defense situation room — a UX study in making dense security telemetry legible, calm, and actionable under pressure.

What you're looking at

NETRADEFEND is a working concept for a security-operations console — one screen where orbital assets, the network perimeter, live attacks, and India's fast-growing cyber-fraud problem sit side by side. Every number here is simulated. The point was never the data; it was the interface — how you make a wall of threat telemetry feel calm enough to actually act on.

Why I built it

I've spent nineteen years designing the connective tissue of product orgs, and the last few chasing how AI and trust should feel on a screen. Security is where that question stops being academic: a dashboard that cries wolf gets ignored, and an ignored alert is how breaches happen. I wanted to design the opposite — a console that earns your attention by spending it carefully.

The CyScam tab is the part I care about most. Digital-arrest scams and UPI fraud are quietly draining ordinary Indian families, and most systems treat it as a line in a spreadsheet. I wanted to give it a map, a face, and the real helpline — 1930 — one tap away.

Built by Puneet Arora — Principal Product Designer, eight granted patents, exploring the UX of AI, trust, and cyber-defense. A portfolio prototype, not a live security product.

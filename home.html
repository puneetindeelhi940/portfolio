§ 02 WHERE AI UX IS HEADING // my working thesis for the next decade
The next decade of design isn’t about drawing screens for AI. It’s about designing with agents in the room as colleagues — and building the operating systems that keep an org coherent while it experiments at speed.

The novelty phase is ending. Users don’t adopt AI because it’s impressive — they adopt it when it’s honest about what it knows and stays useful when it doesn’t. Trust, not cleverness, is the conversion lever. That’s the shift I design for.

I think the interface is dissolving from a place you visit into a continuous, intent-aware collaboration — conversation, canvas, and personalization converging so the product meets you where you already are. The designer’s job moves upstream: from pixels to the rituals, rubrics, and evidence that let judgement scale.

And AI stops being a feature to be designed and becomes a teammate to be onboarded — with prompt libraries, evaluation rubrics, and sandboxes that make the right way the easy way. Build the system that keeps producing good outcomes after you’ve moved on.

§ 04 WHO I AM // the leadership story, told straight

I’m a Principal UX Designer with 19.7 years at the seam between executives, engineers, and end users — from early printer-firmware UIs at H-P R&D, to operations cockpits at Boeing, to GenAI personalization at Dell.com.

Across nearly eight years at Dell (2018–2026) I led the Personalization at Scale practice for the .com — the system where GenAI, behavioural science, analytics, and millions of customers meet. Before that I led the Global UX practice for AI/ML, ecommerce, and CSAT across four product lines and five time zones.

I’m a certified Scrum Master and Prince2 practitioner, with eight granted patents and eight technical publications. I read poetry and listen to spiritual discourses. I work from Bangalore and am open to relocation for the right role.

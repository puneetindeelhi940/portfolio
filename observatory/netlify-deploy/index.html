Why Energy Matters

A single ChatGPT query uses approximately 10x the electricity of a Google search. Training a frontier AI model can consume as much electricity as 1,000 US households use in a year. As AI scales, energy becomes the hidden tax on every AI-touched product and service.

Sources: IEA, Epoch AI, Goldman Sachs Research

Methodology & Data Sources

Every number in this platform is traceable. Here's how we calculate the AI Inflation Composite Index.

Composite Index

The AI Inflation Composite Index is a weighted average of seven components: Technology Hardware (20%), Electricity & Energy (20%), Cloud Services (15%), Consumer Electronics (15%), AI Subscriptions (10%), Enterprise Software (10%), and Infrastructure (10%).

AI Premium Isolation

We separate AI-driven price changes from normal inflation using regression analysis against pre-AI baselines (2019–2022), controlling for supply chain disruptions, currency effects, and seasonal patterns.

Country Scores

Country AI Inflation Scores (0–100) are calculated from: cloud adoption rate, data center density, GPU import dependence, electricity cost, AI startup ecosystem size, government AI spending, and renewable energy share.

Confidence Levels

Every metric carries a confidence score (0–100%). Direct hardware pricing has high confidence (90%+). Second-order and lifestyle impacts have lower confidence (40–70%) due to more complex causal attribution.
